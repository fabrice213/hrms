GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

Learn more about what this HRMS demo can do.

What is Fabrice HRMS?

Fabrice HRMS is a demonstration human resource management system for managing employee records, attendance, leave, vacancies and HR reports.

Can I use it to track attendance and leave?

Yes. An administrator can record employee attendance by date, review a weekly overview, and manage leave requests and their status.

Does the payroll feature send money to workers?

No. The payroll feature prepares local records and can record a payment made outside the app. It does not transfer money or calculate statutory deductions.

Where is information stored?

This is a local demo: employee and HR records are stored in a local JSON file by the Node server, and demo accounts are stored in the browser. It is not designed for real sensitive employee data.

Can I download reports?

Yes. Administrators can open employee, attendance, leave, recruitment and payroll reports in the application, search the displayed records and export them as CSV files.
